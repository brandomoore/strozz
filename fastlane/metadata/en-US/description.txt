Strozz brings live streams and chat together on Apple TV — free and open source.

• Native tvOS experience built for the big screen
• Watch Twitch and YouTube live streams
• Follow supported creators across YouTube and Kick simulcasts
• Watch several channels at once with multi-view
• Live Twitch chat with 7TV, BTTV, and FFZ emote support
• Combine supported Twitch, YouTube, and Kick live chats in a single view
• Incoming raid detection so you can follow the action
• Deep chat appearance customization
• Dark, Light, and OLED themes
