Privacy.

Last updated October 20, 2026

This policy describes the Strozz app and strozz.brando.page. Strozz connects to streaming services to play the content you choose. You can browse public Twitch streams and read chat without signing in.

Data on your device

Strozz stores preferences, cached content, viewing history, and playback progress on your device. Optional recommendations use your viewing history and followed categories. Personalization and history controls are available in the app's settings.

Connected accounts and iCloud

Signing in allows Strozz to access the streaming-service features you authorize, such as your follows and sending chat messages. Account tokens are stored in the device Keychain. Twitch sign-in and an optional Twitch rewards connection sync through encrypted records in your private iCloud database between devices using the same Apple Account. The app's history and saved-progress databases remain device-local; beta diagnostics can include the limited playback metadata described below.

Sign out disconnects the current device. Sign out everywhere signs out Strozz's synced Twitch connections on your other devices when they connect. You can also revoke Strozz's authorization through the streaming service.

Streaming and other services

Strozz requests video, chat, account information, artwork, and emotes from the services you use, including Twitch, YouTube, Kick, 7TV, BTTV, and FFZ, as supported on your device. These providers and their delivery networks receive connection information such as your IP address and the requested content. Signed-in actions are associated with your account at that provider. Optional Twitch rewards features report watch activity to Twitch.

Strozz also downloads shared discovery and creator-alias catalogs to support recommendations and simulcasts. Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies.

Diagnostics and beta feedback

The app produces local diagnostic logs for troubleshooting. If you choose to share logs, screenshots, or feedback, the information you send is used to investigate the report. Review it before sharing, and do not post account tokens or other private information in public issues.

TestFlight builds with automatic diagnostics send playback incidents, crash reports, and lightweight stream-health summaries to Sentry without a separate opt-in. Reports include public channel names, viewing timestamps, platform/build, quality, buffer and frame/clock progress, structured error codes, and recovery actions. Each stream has a random diagnostic session identifier, including in multiview. App Store builds do not enable this automatic remote reporting.

Diagnostic reports exclude chat text and participants, Twitch or Apple account identities, credentials, signed stream URLs, request headers, screenshots, and audio/video recordings. Client filtering and project scrubbing limit identifying data. Sentry receives network connection information to accept reports; the project is configured to prevent storing IP addresses and scrub user/geography fields. See Sentry's privacy policy.

Local diagnostic history and offline upload queues are bounded and purgeable. Repeated incidents are coalesced with counts. Reports may be delayed or lost when a device is offline, its cache is reclaimed, or a service quota is reached; collection is not a complete viewing record.

TestFlight is operated by Apple. Apple handles beta participation and may share crash information, usage information, and feedback with the developer under its TestFlight privacy terms.

This website

This website does not use analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or account cookies. Cloudflare hosts the site and processes web requests, including IP addresses and request metadata, to deliver and protect it. Links to TestFlight and GitHub take you to those services.

The homepage loads five chat-emote images directly from FrankerFaceZ's delivery network at cdn.frankerfacez.com. These requests share your IP address and ordinary connection metadata with that provider. The image requests omit cookies and the referring page URL. The Party Parrot illustration is hosted with this website.

A small script makes the homepage emotes react to your cursor and clicks. These interactions stay in your browser; they are not recorded or transmitted. Clicking can load additional reaction images from the same FFZ image server described above. The growing emote scene is not saved and resets when you reload the page.

Questions and changes

Contact the maintainer through the Strozz GitHub project for questions about the app or this policy. This page will be updated when these practices change.
